The Greece Island Starter

Naxos  The underrated one.

Best beaches in the Cyclades, the marble Portara at sunset, half the crowds.

Paros  The sweet spot and your hub.

Whitewashed Naoussa, easy pace, ferries that reach everywhere else.

Mykonos  For colour and courage.

Windmills, white walls, the red-dress shot. Go bold, leave before it roars.

Santorini  The postcard.

The caldera sunset. Book Oia dinner early, stay a night past the day-trippers.

Crete  A whole country.

Chania's harbour, pink-sand beaches, mountain villages. Give it its own week.

THE HACK
One Cyclades ferry route links Mykonos, Paros, Naxos and Santorini. Base in Paros and day-trip the rest. Add the mainland later: Meteora and Thessaloniki. Go May to mid-June or September for warm sea and thinner crowds.

The Permission Slip

1. Pick the date before the place.

A trip with a date is a plan. A someday is a wish.

2. Name your number.

Decide what it's worth and write it down. A real number quiets the fear.

3. Choose your one non-negotiable.

The single thing that makes the trip yours. Build around that yes.

4. Say it out loud to one person.

Said out loud, a plan is harder to quietly cancel.

5. Book the one thing you can't undo.

The flight, or the first night. That's the step that makes it real.
